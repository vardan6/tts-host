eSpeak NG 1.52.0 (espeak-ng.exe, libespeak-ng.dll, espeak-ng-data/)

TTS Host ships the unmodified Windows binaries from the official eSpeak NG
1.52.0 release (espeak-ng.msi) beside the Kokoro runner, which runs
espeak-ng.exe as a separate process to convert text to phonemes.
